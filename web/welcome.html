Questions, answered.

Does it work for night shifts and other time zones?

Yes. Shifts can cross midnight and still count for the day they began, and each person can have their own time zone and hours.

What do the screenshots actually capture?

The screen in use, 1 to 3 times every 10 minutes at random moments, only while the timer runs. Private windows and notifications are blacked out first, on the employee’s own computer.

Can it run our payroll?

It works out each payslip from tracked hours, attendance, time off, expenses and income tax, including people paid in dollars. You approve it and mark each person paid.

Will my team push back on being tracked?

They see everything that’s tracked about them, they’re asked before tracking starts, and they’re told about every change. That openness is the point.

How long does it take to set up?

Import people from their contracts, set your shift hours, and send everyone the desktop app. We help you through it.
