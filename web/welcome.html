Tracking people can see.

Most trackers spy. Staffwarden is built the other way round, so your team trusts it and you trust the numbers.

  • Everyone sees their own

    Hours, screenshots, who opened them. Nothing hidden from the person it’s about.

  • Private windows blacked out

    WhatsApp, banking, password managers and pop-ups are painted black on their computer before a screenshot leaves it.

  • Nothing while paused

    No screenshots on a break, when idle or after Stop. Ever.

  • Told about every change

    Change someone’s schedule or time and they hear about it, with your name on it.

  • They can delete their own

    People can remove their screenshots, and old ones go after 60 days.

  • Asked first

    Nobody is tracked until they’ve read the policy and said yes.

Questions, answered.

Does it work across time zones and different shifts?

Yes. Each person can have their own hours and time zone, from early mornings to overnight shifts. Shifts can even cross midnight and still count for the day they began, and each person can have their own time zone and hours.

What do the screenshots actually capture?

The screen in use, 1 to 3 times every 10 minutes at random moments, only while the timer runs. Private windows and notifications are blacked out first, on the employee’s own computer.

Can it run our payroll?

It works out each payslip from tracked hours, attendance, time off, expenses and income tax, including people paid in dollars. You approve it and mark each person paid.

Will my team push back on being tracked?

They see everything that’s tracked about them, they’re asked before tracking starts, and they’re told about every change. That openness is the point.

How long does it take to set up?

Import people from their contracts, set your shift hours, and send everyone the desktop app. We help you through it.
